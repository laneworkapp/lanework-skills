The background loop that renews a lease and listens for a takeover notice has been called a "timer" on this board. The owner pointed out it is a control loop and has little to do with time. What is it called, in the records and the glossary?

## Options

- A: Keeper. The process that keeps a holder's lease: it renews, listens for a takeover notice, and exits with its session.
- B: Control loop, the owner's words.
- C: Timer, as written so far.

## Recommended

A. One short word that says what the process is for. "Control loop" is a general programming term, and "timer" misleads. Either way the other words go under Avoid.
